Champagne isn’t only for New Year’s — and soft goat cheese isn’t only for salads. Together they’re light, lively, and a little luxurious: the kind of pairing that makes a regular evening feel like a celebration.

Think citrusy bubbles meeting creamy tang. Instant bestie energy.

## Why this pairing works

Soft goat cheese (chèvre-style logs or rounds) is bright and lactic. Champagne (or a dry traditional-method sparkler) brings acidity, fine bubbles, and toasty notes. The fizz scrubs the palate; the cheese softens the wine’s zip. Fresh, clean, totally snackable.

## Easy ways to serve

- **Brunch board:** goat cheese, berries, baguette, a little jam.
- **Aperitif hour:** just cheese, crackers, and bubbles — done.
- **Herb glow-up:** roll the log in cracked pepper or chives before slicing.

## Bestie tip

No Champagne on hand? A dry Cava or Crémant keeps the same vibe. The rule is bubbles + brightness, not the price tag.

**Pop, spread, cheers.** ✨🧀
