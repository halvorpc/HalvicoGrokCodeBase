## Body (paste as HTML)

<p>Some pairings feel like they’ve been besties forever. Cabernet Sauvignon and aged cheddar? That’s one of them. Deep fruit, firm tannins, and a cheddar with real bite — they don’t compete; they finish each other’s sentences.</p>

<p>If you want a board that looks grown-up and still feels totally approachable, start here.</p>

<h2>Why this pairing works</h2>
<p>Aged cheddar brings salty, nutty intensity. Cabernet brings dark berries, a little oak spice, and structure. The cheese softens the wine’s tannins; the wine cuts through the cheese’s richness. Result: every bite resets your palate for the next sip.</p>

<div>
  <h2>Pairing callout</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Wine:</strong> Cabernet Sauvignon (medium- to full-bodied; California, Bordeaux-style, or your favorite regional bottle)</li>
    <li><strong>Cheese:</strong> Aged cheddar (12–24 months if you can find it — sharper is better)</li>
    <li><strong>Why it works:</strong> Tannins + fat = harmony. Cab’s structure stands up to cheddar’s intensity; cheddar rounds out the wine’s edges.</li>
    <li><strong>Serving tips:</strong> Serve cheddar near room temperature (pull it 30–45 minutes early). Pour Cab slightly cooler than room temp. Add crackers, toasted walnuts, and a drizzle of honey for contrast.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h2>Build the board</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Cube or shard the cheddar so guests get that crystalline crunch.</li>
  <li>Add something sweet (fig jam or honey) and something crunchy (almonds or rustic crackers).</li>
  <li>Keep the wine pours modest — this duo is rich; small tastes go far.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Bestie tip</h2>
<p>Hosting? Label the cheese “Aged cheddar — Cab’s favorite.” People remember the story as much as the flavor.</p>

<p><strong>Cheers to classic besties.</strong> 🍷🧀</p>
