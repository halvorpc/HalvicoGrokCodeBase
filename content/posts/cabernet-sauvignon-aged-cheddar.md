Some pairings feel like they’ve been besties forever. Cabernet Sauvignon and aged cheddar? That’s one of them. Deep fruit, firm tannins, and a cheddar with real bite — they don’t compete; they finish each other’s sentences.

If you want a board that looks grown-up and still feels totally approachable, start here.

## Why this pairing works

Aged cheddar brings salty, nutty intensity. Cabernet brings dark berries, a little oak spice, and structure. The cheese softens the wine’s tannins; the wine cuts through the cheese’s richness. Result: every bite resets your palate for the next sip.

## Build the board

- Cube or shard the cheddar so guests get that crystalline crunch.
- Add something sweet (fig jam or honey) and something crunchy (almonds or rustic crackers).
- Keep the wine pours modest — this duo is rich; small tastes go far.

## Bestie tip

Hosting? Label the cheese “Aged cheddar — Cab’s favorite.” People remember the story as much as the flavor.
