If Cab + cheddar is the reliable bestie, Port + blue is the one who shows up in velvet. Intensely sweet wine, intensely salty cheese — they shouldn’t work, and yet they absolutely do.

This is your after-dinner flex: small pours, big flavor, zero fuss.

## Why this pairing works

Blue cheese is salty, funky, and creamy. Port is sweet, fortified, and full of dried fruit and spice. Salt amplifies sweetness; sweetness tames the blue’s punch. It’s contrast that clicks — like sweet-and-salty snacks, but elevated.

## Keep it simple

- One blue, one Port, one crunchy element (walnuts or water crackers).
- Offer a milder blue if guests are new to the style — Gorgonzola Dolce is a friendly on-ramp.
- Save this for the end of the night when people are ready to linger.

## Bestie tip

Tell the table: “Sweet wine, salty cheese — trust the plot twist.” Watching skeptics convert is half the fun.
