If your flavor personality is “bright and breezy,” this is your signature duo. Sauvignon Blanc’s citrus-and-herb snap meets fresh chèvre’s clean tang — like a picnic that packed itself.

It’s the pairing we reach for when the weather’s warm, the playlist is easy, and nobody wants anything heavy.

## Why this pairing works

Fresh chèvre is lemony and light. Sauvignon Blanc (think Loire, New Zealand, or crisp California) brings grapefruit, grass, and mouthwatering acidity. Matching intensity + matching freshness = a combo that feels effortless and tastes intentional.

## Serve it your way

- **Picnic mode:** pre-slice baguette, pack the chèvre, freeze a cold pack next to the bottle.
- **Herby glow-up:** top chèvre with lemon zest, cracked pepper, and thyme.
- **Honey drizzle:** a little sweet against the tang makes people go “ohhh.”

## Bestie tip

This is a fantastic “starter pairing” for friends who say they don’t like goat cheese. The wine’s citrus makes the chèvre taste friendlier — promise.
